# Gemma Garden Guardian Video Design

## Mood

Practical, trustworthy, hopeful, field-ready, and polished enough for a Kaggle project demo.

## Palette

- Background: `#07100b`
- Panel: `#0d1c14`
- Foreground: `#f7fbf6`
- Muted text: `#b9c8bc`
- Primary accent: `#62d27e`
- Secondary accent: `#7dd3fc`
- Caution accent: `#ffd166`
- Tomato accent: `#f25f4c`
- Soil accent: `#8b6548`

## Typography

- Primary font: `Inter`, falling back to system sans-serif
- Headlines: heavy, direct, 72px to 104px
- Body: readable, calm, 30px to 42px
- Labels: compact, high-contrast, 20px to 28px

## Motion

- Slow camera push on garden imagery
- Structured scene holds for narration
- Scan-line and observation marker motion for AI analysis moments
- Avoid frantic transitions; the video should feel confident and demo-ready

## Constraints

- No pesticide imagery
- No definitive disease diagnosis wording
- Use uncertainty-aware language such as possible risks, visible signs, and recommended checks
- The real Streamlit demo segment must be easy to replace later
